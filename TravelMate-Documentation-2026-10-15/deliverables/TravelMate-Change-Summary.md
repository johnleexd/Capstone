# TravelMate documentation change summary

Version 2.0 — 15 October 2026. Originals were not overwritten.

## Platform

Old: PHP/PDO, MySQL, XAMPP

Updated: Next.js/React, Express, Prisma/PostgreSQL; current source governs.

## Geographic scope

Old: Cebu-focused; fixed seven-day statements

Updated: Worldwide source-backed discovery; 1–31 inclusive days; explicit catalogue limits.

## Roles

Old: Owner/marketplace/admin-content intentions

Updated: Current traveler/admin plus guest state and suspended appeal; legacy schema labeled inactive.

## Generation

Old: OpenAI-only/general AI narrative

Updated: Configurable OpenAI/Gemini constrained ID/time schedule, one repair/batch, deterministic reconciliation and catalog fallback.

## Money

Old: Fixed percentage allocation/final pricing implications

Updated: Group ceiling, 20 currencies, explicit estimate provenance, minor units, contingency and unpriced gaps.

## Accommodation/travel offers

Old: Booking/payment and provider comparison descriptions

Updated: Current suggestion-only stay UI; quote/comparison API-only compatibility; no commercial booking or payment.

## Saved trips

Old: Incomplete version/lifecycle description

Updated: Owned autosave/idempotency, manual drafts, full-envelope versions, archive/complete/delete and separate actual visits.

## Maps/day management

Old: Standalone GPS and removed workspace tabs

Updated: Saved-trip maps, exact-stop resolution, opt-in GPS, managed activities and preview/accept replan.

## Authentication

Old: Six-digit OTP/JWT or refresh assumptions

Updated: Eight-character verification, salted scrypt, custom signed eight-hour session, revocation/sessionVersion and safe OAuth.

## Administration

Old: Prompt editor/catalog or broad ownership claims

Updated: User status/warnings, reports/appeals, feedback, notifications, SSE, records/config visibility only.

## Performance/privacy

Old: Unverified uptime/load/encryption/backup claims

Updated: Controls versus proposed targets; privacy gaps, backup/recovery and UAT remain explicit.

## Management

Old: Old dates, roles and PHP 2000 proposal

Updated: Archived proposed baseline; current names, year, dates, assignments and costs use labeled placeholders.

## Evidence

Old: Feature names or old screenshots treated as completion

Updated: 93-feature inventory; route/page/process reconciliation; 113 requirements and 133 Not-executed acceptance cases.

## Testing

Old: Unsupported blanket pass statements

Updated: 152 frontend/521 backend isolated passes; historical reports separate; live persistence/providers/UAT not claimed.

## Public globe

Old: Older permanent panel/arcs/markers and image-only phase

Updated: Current rounded photo plus English country name/flag, hover border, exact-code identity and explicit area coverage gap.

## Document structure

Old: Separate incomplete old SRS/SPMP

Updated: Reference-based manuscript with engineering documents/manual/front matter, diagrams, registers and unresolved list.
